I'm a junior full stack developer from Belgium, wrapping up a master's in computer science at the Université libre de Bruxelles (ULB) with a specialisation in artificial intelligence.

I'm looking for my first junior developer role, ideally in the Odoo ecosystem, where I can keep learning from experienced colleagues and contribute from day one.
